GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before we get started?

Here are a few basics to help you decide whether we’re a good fit.

Ask me a question ↗
Which businesses do you work with? +

I’m interested in supporting small businesses, e-commerce stores, fashion and beauty brands, startups, and service-based businesses. We can discuss your goals to see what support makes sense.

Do you guarantee Google rankings or ad results? +

No. SEO and advertising outcomes depend on many factors. I focus on a clear process, relevant work, and honest communication rather than guaranteed results.

How does a project begin? +

Start by sharing your business, goals, and the service you’re interested in through the contact page. We can then discuss scope, timeline, and next steps.

Can I ask about a custom package? +

Yes. Share the channels you use, your goals, and the support you need. Deliverables and pricing can be discussed before work begins.
